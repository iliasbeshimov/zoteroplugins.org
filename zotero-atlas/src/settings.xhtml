Find and install plugins from zoteroplugins.org inside Zotero, filtered to your Zotero version, with each plugin's grade. It also looks after the plugins you have: it tells you when one is blocked or has a known security problem, drops a grade, or gets an update graded lower, and shows each plugin's grade in Tools → Plugins. Open it from Tools → Zotero Atlas, or the Zotero Atlas button by the sync button.
