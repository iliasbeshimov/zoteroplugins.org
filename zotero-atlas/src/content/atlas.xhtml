Zotero Atlas

Finds plugins for Zotero, and looks after the ones you have.

What it does

  • Find plugins. Say what you want Zotero to do, and see the plugins that work with your Zotero, each with its grade from zoteroplugins.org and what it needs. Install puts in the exact file that was checked.
  • Your plugins. Tells you when a plugin is blocked, has a known security problem, drops a grade or gets an update graded lower. It can hold each update until it's been checked (the setting below), and lets you go back to the version you had.
  • Tools → Plugins. Shows each plugin's grade beside it.
  • Move to another computer. Saves your plugins, and their settings if you like, to a file that Zotero Atlas on the other computer installs from.
  • Ratings. After you've used a plugin for a couple of weeks, asks how it's working. Nothing is sent until you click Post.
