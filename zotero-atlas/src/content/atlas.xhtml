Take the plugins you have here to Zotero on another computer: save a list of them here, then open it in Zotero Atlas there.

The file stays on your computer. Zotero Atlas sends nothing about it anywhere. You move it yourself, by USB stick, email or a shared folder.

On this computer: save the list

Settings whose names look like keys, tokens or passwords are left out unless you tick this. If you do, keep the file private.

On the other computer: open the list

Zotero Atlas shows what it would install, then installs only files zoteroplugins.org has checked, from GitHub, the same way it installs from Find plugins.
